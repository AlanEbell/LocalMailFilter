Allow-list


Senders you rescued. A match skips the model entirely — except for confident phishing, which is still flagged even for trusted senders, since impersonation is how phishing works.





Repair


Versions up to 0.1.18 had the report's Agree button record the opposite of what you meant: it rewrote the verdict to business spam and logged a correction claiming the model had missed the message. Those corrections are fed back into the prompt, so they teach it the wrong lesson. This removes them and the affected verdicts, leaving your allow-list alone. The messages are simply classified again on the next scan.



   

   



Re-classify


Discards the model's verdicts and clears its tags so mail can be judged again — useful after a classifier fix. Your allow-list and corrections are kept; those are your judgements, not the model's, and re-earning them would waste your review work.



  
  
  
  
  
Afterwards, open the report tab and press Scan to re-classify.



Backup


Uninstalling the add-on discards the allow-list and everything it has learned — Thunderbird gives a reinstalled add-on a fresh, empty storage area. Installing a newer version over the top keeps it. Export before you uninstall.
